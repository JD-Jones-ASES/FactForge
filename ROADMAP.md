## Optional later

- Parallel-line “name the relationship” (choice chips)
- Polynomial long division / remainder theorem
- Rational expressions (simplify, add) and rational equations
- Exponential growth / decay evaluation
- Trig equations on [0, 2π) and sum/difference identities
- Limits of rational functions at a point (pre-calc bridge)
- More packs only when a clear curriculum gap appears

## Deliberately out of scope

Backend, accounts, spaced-repetition servers, full CAS, drag-to-measure geometry.
